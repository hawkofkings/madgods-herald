Ah, welcome. His Grace has been expecting you. I am Haskill, chamberlain to the Madgod, and it falls upon me to oversee your little... examination.

Nine questions await you. Nine opportunities to demonstrate where your particular brand of madness belongs. Will you find your place beneath the golden skies of Mania, or among the shadowed depths of Dementia?

A Trial of the Mind

The Madgod's Herald invites you to undertake nine trials. Each answer reveals a little more about your nature, your instincts, and the realm that calls to you.

Please, do try to answer honestly. His Grace does so enjoy a surprise, but even he appreciates a little order in the proceedings.

Two Realms. One Destiny.

The Isles are divided between two contrasting realms. Your answers will reveal which one has laid claim to you.

☀

THE GOLDEN REALM OF MANIA

Brilliance, passion, creativity, ambition, and boundless imagination. Those who embrace the extraordinary may find themselves drawn toward Mania.

☾

THE SHADOWED REALM OF DEMENTIA

Shadows, contemplation, mystery, caution, and hidden truths. Those who find meaning in the darkness may discover their place in Dementia.

BEGIN THE SORTING CEREMONY

Your realm is yours once assigned. You cannot retake the ordinary ceremony or change your house yourself. A new sorting is possible only when the server's administrator declares the Greymarch.

A DECREE FROM HIS GRACE

THE GREYMARCH

Even the order of the Shivering Isles may be undone. When the server's administrator invokes the Greymarch, the old house assignments are cleared and a new sorting begins.

“Attention, inhabitants of the Shivering Isles. By decree of His Grace, the Madgod, the boundaries between Mania and Dementia shall once again be undone. Your former allegiances are no more. The realms stand open, and the time has come for a new reckoning. The old sorting is over. A new trial awaits those brave enough to face it.”

— HASKILL, CHAMBERLAIN TO THE MADGOD

During the Greymarch, Haskill announces the event in Discord, existing Mania and Dementia roles are removed, and eligible members may undertake the active Greymarch question set. The alternate questions will be revealed when the event is prepared.

THE GREYMARCH IS AN ADMINISTRATOR-ACTIVATED EVENT
