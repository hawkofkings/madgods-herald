# The Madgod's Herald — Privacy Policy **Effective Date:** November 2, 2026 **Last Updated:** November 2, 2026 ### 1. Introduction The Madgod's Herald ("the Bot") is a Discord application inspired by The Shivering Isles from The Elder Scrolls series. This Privacy Policy explains how the Bot collects, uses, stores, and handles information when Discord users interact with its sorting quiz and related features. We are committed to collecting only the information necessary to operate the Bot. ### 2. Information We Collect When users interact with the sorting quiz, the Bot may collect and store the following information: **Discord Account Information** - Discord User ID. - Discord Server (Guild) ID. **Quiz Information** - Answers selected for each of the nine sorting questions. - Mania and Dementia point totals. - Quiz completion status. - Final assigned house. **Technical Information** The Bot may process interaction information supplied by Discord that is necessary to respond to button clicks and other supported interactions. We do not intentionally collect Discord passwords, private messages, payment information, or personal financial information. ### 3. How We Use Information Collected information is used exclusively for operating and maintaining the Bot. This includes: - Recording quiz responses. - Tracking quiz progress. - Calculating Mania and Dementia scores. - Determining the participant's final house. - Assigning the appropriate Discord role. - Supporting quiz retakes. - Troubleshooting technical problems and preventing abuse. We do not use quiz responses to create advertising profiles or sell personal information. ### 4. Data Storage and Retention Quiz information may be stored in a database to preserve progress and results between Bot restarts. Our intended retention practices are: - Active quiz responses are retained while a participant is completing the quiz. - Completed quiz results may be retained to support role management and retakes. - Previous quiz responses are replaced when a participant starts a new attempt. - Users may request deletion of their stored quiz information. We will retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal requirements. ### 5. Information Sharing We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Information may be processed by Discord as part of the platform's normal operation. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect the security and integrity of the Bot. We do not intentionally share quiz responses with unrelated third parties. ### 6. Data Security Reasonable technical and administrative measures will be used to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no electronic storage system or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. ### 7. User Data Deletion Requests Users may request deletion of their stored quiz information. To request deletion, contact the Bot administrator using the contact information below. Requests will be reviewed and processed within a reasonable timeframe, subject to applicable legal requirements. Deleting stored quiz information does not automatically remove Discord roles or erase information independently retained by Discord. ### 8. Children's Privacy The Bot is intended for users who are permitted to use Discord under its applicable age requirements and local laws. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws. If you believe a child has provided information inappropriately, please contact the Bot administrator. ### 9. Third-Party Services The Bot operates through Discord and uses Discord's application and interaction systems. Your use of Discord remains subject to Discord's own policies: Discord Privacy Policy: https://discord.com/privacy The Madgod's Herald is not affiliated with or endorsed by Discord. ### 10.
